My product is called Dotmole, a domain finder. Return two groups of candidates in one pass: one that keeps the full brand name with suitable TLDs, and another that uses wordplay, spelling, compounds, or domain hacks such as digfor.me. Draw from ideas like dot, mole, dig, and domain. Avoid hyphens and numbers. Prioritize names that are short, easy to say and spell, and not premium. Check availability live, recommend only options explicitly available for registration, rank them by group, explain the naming idea and TLD constraints, then choose a primary domain and the best creative alternative.
