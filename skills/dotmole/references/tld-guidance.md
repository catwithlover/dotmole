# TLD Guidance

Use this as a relevance guide, not as a substitute for current registry eligibility rules. The user's explicit preferences always take priority.

## Common intent mappings

| Intent | Useful starting TLDs |
| --- | --- |
| General business or global brand | `.com`, `.co`, `.net` |
| Software engineering or developer tool | `.dev`, `.app`, `.sh`, `.build`, `.software`, `.tools`, `.codes` |
| Cloud, infrastructure, or systems | `.cloud`, `.systems`, `.network`, `.io`, `.dev` |
| AI or data product | `.ai`, `.io`, `.dev`, `.cloud` |
| Open source or community | `.org`, `.community`, `.dev`, `.social` |
| Personal site or memorable phrase | `.me`, `.life`, `.world`, `.page` |
| Commerce | `.shop`, `.store`, `.market`, `.com` |
| Creative work | `.design`, `.studio`, `.art`, `.works` |

Start with 5 to 8 highly relevant TLDs. More extensions do not automatically produce better recommendations.

## Naming patterns

- Exact seed plus a relevant TLD: `dotmole.dev`
- Clear compound: `dotmolelabs.com`, `dotmoletools.dev`
- Action prefix: `getdotmole.com`, `usedotmole.dev`
- Natural phrase: `digwith.me`
- Product role: `dotmole.build`, `dotmole.tools`
- Carefully chosen domain hack where the extension completes the phrase

Prefer names that can be spoken once and typed correctly. Avoid hyphens, numbers, and deliberate misspellings unless the user asks for them or all clearer options are unavailable.

## Important caveats

- `.app` and `.dev` are HSTS-preloaded namespaces, so deployed websites require HTTPS.
- `.sh` is the country-code TLD for Saint Helena. Its shell association is conventional, not its registry purpose.
- `.io`, `.ai`, and `.me` are country-code TLDs used globally; policies and pricing can differ from generic TLDs.
- `.cat` is a sponsored namespace associated with the Catalan linguistic and cultural community, not a generic extension for cat-related sites.
- Geographic, professional, and sponsored TLDs may have eligibility or content requirements. Do not claim eligibility without checking current registry rules.

## Risk checks

Flag obvious trademark conflicts, confusing similarity, unexpected double meanings, and hard-to-hear spellings. This is a naming screen, not legal clearance. Never claim that a recommendation is trademark-safe.
